## 2. Discount Approval Matrix & Hard Floor

Sales representatives may offer volume or multi-year discounts strictly within the approval hierarchy below (`BETA-DEAL-403`):
* **0% to 10% Discount (`<= 10%`)**: Account Executive (AE) self-approved in CRM.
* **10.1% to 20% Discount (`10% < d <= 20%`)**: Requires **Sales Director** approval (`SL-DISC-20`).
* **20.1% to 30% Discount (`20% < d <= 30%`)**: Requires **VP of Sales + CFO** co-approval.
* **Hard Floor Rule**: Discounts **above 30% are strictly prohibited** under all circumstances—even for multi-year enterprise deals.

## 3. SLA Guarantees & Forbidden Commercial Promises

### 3.1 SLA Credit Schedule (Zero Cash Refunds)
* If monthly uptime for **Pro (< 99.5%)** or **Enterprise (< 99.95%)** drops below guarantee:
  * Below SLA threshold down to 99.0%: **10% service credit** applied to the next billing invoice.
  * Below 99.0%: **25% service credit** applied to the next billing invoice.
* **Strict No-Cash-Refund Policy**: Beta Inc. **never** issues cash refunds for SLA downtime; compensation is exclusively in the form of future invoice credits.

### 3.2 Prohibited Clauses
Sales representatives must **never** promise:
1. **99.99% uptime** or custom penalty clauses without a signed Legal + CTO addendum (`SL-CUSTOM-99`);
2. Unlimited free onboarding or custom feature development commitments inside standard order forms;
3. Monthly cancellation rights on Enterprise annual contracts.
