## 1. Leveling & 14-Month Compensation Structure

### 1.1 Base Salary Bands (Monthly, Pre-Tax, USD)
Beta Inc. uses an **`L3`–`L6`** leveling framework with a **guaranteed 14-month annual base structure** (12 regular monthly payrolls plus 1 fixed mid-year month in June and 1 fixed year-end month in December, prorated by service days in that calendar year).

| Level | Typical Role | Monthly Base Min (USD) | Monthly Base Max (USD) | Annual Guaranteed (14 mo) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L3** | Associate / Junior Engineer | 4,500 | 6,500 | 63,000 – 91,000 |
| **L4** | Engineer / Specialist | 6,500 | 9,500 | 91,000 – 133,000 |
| **L5** | Senior / Tech Lead | 9,500 | 13,500 | 133,000 – 189,000 |
| **L6** | Principal / Department Head | 13,500 | 19,000 | 189,000 – 266,000 |

* **Probation Rule**: Consistent with the *Beta Employee Handbook*, employees undergo a **6-month probation period**. New hires still in probation on June 30 or December 31 receive their 14th-month payout prorated to exact completed days, provided they pass probation.

## 2. Performance Reviews (Numeric 1–5 Scale, No Forced Distribution)

### 2.1 Rating Scale & Review Cycle
Beta conducts **semi-annual** performance reviews in **June and December** using a **1-to-5 numeric scale**. Unlike forced-ranking systems, Beta enforces **no mandatory forced distribution** across ratings:
* **5 (Outstanding)**: Exceptional impact; eligible for **2.0 months** additional performance bonus + up to 12% merit raise.
* **4 (Exceeds Expectations)**: Strong delivery; eligible for **1.0 month** additional performance bonus + 6%–8% merit raise.
* **3 (Meets Expectations)**: Solid performance; eligible for **0.5 month** additional performance bonus + 3%–5% merit raise.
* **2 (Developing)**: Below level bar; **0** performance bonus and ineligible for merit raise.
* **1 (Unsatisfactory)**: Immediate performance concern; **0** performance bonus.

### 2.2 Coaching & Review Plan (CRP)
* Any employee receiving a **Rating `1` once**, or **Rating `2` in two consecutive semi-annual cycles**, enters a **45-day Coaching & Review Plan (CRP)** (`BETA-HR-CRP01`).
* During CRP, internal transfers are frozen until HR confirms successful completion.

## 3. Severance & Offboarding Rules

### 3.1 Statutory `N` Formula (No Extra `+1` When Notice Is Served)
For involuntary separations, Beta pays **statutory `N` severance** (where the company normally provides **30 days' advance working notice**, so **no extra `+1` notice-in-lieu month** is paid unless immediate departure is requested by management):
* **Tenure Rounding (`N`)**: Completed years of service, where partial years **under 6 months count as `0.5`**, and **6 months or more count as `1.0`**.
* **Leave Handling**: Consistent with the *Beta Employee Handbook*, unused annual leave is **not cashed out**; impacted employees are scheduled to consume remaining leave balances during their 30-day notice period.

### 3.2 Worked Calculation Example
* **Scenario**: An L4 Engineer earning **$8,000 USD/month** with **4 years and 3 months** of tenure is impacted by restructuring and given full 30 days' advance notice.
* **Calculation**: Because 3 months is `< 6 months`, `N = 4 + 0.5 = 4.5`. Since 30-day notice is served, there is no `+1`. Total severance = `4.5 × $8,000 = $36,000 USD`.
