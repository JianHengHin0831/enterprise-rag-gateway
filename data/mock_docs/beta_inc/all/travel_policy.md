# Beta Inc. Business Travel Policy (2026 Edition)

All Beta business travel is booked through the travel portal `travel.beta.example`. Questions go to `travel-desk@beta-inc.example`.

---

## 1. Pre-Approval

* Domestic trips need manager approval in the portal **7 days** before departure.
* International trips need manager and department head approval **21 days** before departure.
* Interns may travel only with a full-time employee from their team.

---

## 2. Flights & Trains

* Economy class for all flights under **6 hours**. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more; business class requires CFO approval.
* Trains are preferred over flights for trips under **4 hours** door to door.

---

## 3. Hotels

| City tier | Nightly cap (USD) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tier 1 (major hubs) | 260 |
| Tier 2 | 180 |
| Tier 3 | 130 |

* Stays above the cap need manager approval in the portal before booking.

---

## 4. Per Diem

* Domestic per diem is **55 USD per day**; international per diem is **85 USD per day**.
* Per diem replaces itemized meal receipts. Meals provided by a conference or client are deducted at **15 USD each**.

---

## 5. Expense Submission

* Submit travel expenses within **30 days** of return. Claims submitted later than 60 days are rejected.
* Lost receipts above 25 USD need a signed missing-receipt declaration.
