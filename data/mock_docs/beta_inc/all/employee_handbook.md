# Beta Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

Welcome to Beta Inc. This handbook outlines standard company policies for attendance, leave, expense reimbursement, and workplace conduct across all Beta Inc teams.

---

## 1. Working Hours & Attendance Policy

### 1.1 Fixed Working Schedule
Beta Inc uses **fixed working hours**: **09:00–18:00**, Monday through Friday, with a **1-hour lunch break from 12:00 to 13:00**.
* **Punctuality**: Badge check-in is recorded at the office turnstile. Arriving after 09:15 more than three times in a calendar month without prior notice counts as half a day of unpaid leave.
* **Wednesday Company Sync**: Every Wednesday morning (10:00–12:00) is reserved for Beta's mandatory **All-Hands & Cross-Team Sprint Planning**—all staff are expected to be onsite or actively present on video calls.

### 1.2 Onsite & Remote Work Policy
* Beta Inc operates as an **onsite-first** workplace. Routine weekly remote work is not offered.
* Full-time employees who have completed probation may request up to **1 ad-hoc work-from-home (WFH) day per month** for personal errands or utility repairs, subject to manager approval at least 24 hours in advance.

### 1.3 Overtime Policy
Overtime requires written manager pre-approval in the Beta Ops Portal (`ops.beta-inc.example`). Approved weekday overtime after 20:00 or weekend work is compensated as **1:1 compensatory leave** (must be redeemed within **30 days**).

---

## 2. Leave & Holiday Entitlements

### 2.1 Annual Leave
* **Day-One Eligibility**: Full-time employees get **5 days of paid annual leave from their first day** of employment.
* **Tenure Progression**: After completing 3 full years of service, annual leave increases to **8 days** per year, capped at a maximum of **10 days** per year after 5 years.
* **No Carryover ("Use It or Lose It")**: Unused annual leave **cannot be carried over** to the next calendar year and expires on **December 31** (no cash payout upon resignation).
* **Interns**: Interns receive **0 paid annual leave days**, but may take up to 2 days of approved unpaid exam/school leave per quarter.

### 2.2 Sick & Personal Leave
* **Sick Leave**: Must be logged in the Ops Portal before **09:30** on the morning of absence. **Any sick leave (even 1 day)** requires an outpatient clinic receipt or medical note attached upon return. Paid sick leave is capped at **3 days per year** at 80% base pay.
* **Personal Leave**: Unpaid personal leave may be requested in half-day increments with 3 business days' advance approval.

---

## 3. Expense Reimbursement Policy

### 3.1 Strict 15-Day Submission Deadline
* All business expenses must be submitted in the Ops Portal **within 15 calendar days** of the transaction date. Claims submitted on Day 16 or later are permanently locked out by the system.
* Approved claims are settled via bi-weekly payroll runs on the 1st and 15th of each month.

### 3.2 Approval Thresholds
* **Up to $1,000 USD** (`<= 1,000`): Approved by the direct manager.
* **$1,000.01 to $5,000 USD** (`1,000 < amount <= 5,000`): Requires direct manager + department head approval.
* **Above $5,000 USD** (`> 5,000`): Requires **CEO approval** in addition to department head sign-off.

### 3.3 Travel & Meal Rules
* **Hotel Cap**: Flat limit of **$150 USD per night** nationwide (no tier-1 city differential).
* **Meal Allowance**: Up to **$30 USD per day** while traveling, and **itemized receipts are strictly required** for every meal claim (no flat per diem without receipts).
* **Overtime Taxi**: Employees working approved overtime past **21:30** may claim up to **$30 USD** per trip with an official e-receipt.
