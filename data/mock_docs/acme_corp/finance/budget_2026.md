# Acme FY2026 Budget Controls (Restricted: Finance)

> **Confidential — Finance only.** All figures are fictional.

---

## 1. FY2026 Operating Budget by Department (USD)

| Department | Annual budget | Quarterly release |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Engineering | 18,400,000 | 25% per quarter |
| Sales & Marketing | 9,600,000 | 30% / 25% / 25% / 20% |
| G&A (HR, Finance, Legal) | 4,200,000 | 25% per quarter |
| Facilities | 2,100,000 | 25% per quarter |

* Unspent quarterly budget rolls over to the next quarter **within the same fiscal year only**.

---

## 2. Variance Rules

* A department forecast to exceed its quarterly release by more than **5%** must file a variance memo with the FP&A team before the 10th of the following month.
* Overruns above **10%** of the annual budget require CFO approval and a remediation plan.

---

## 3. Purchase Commitments

* Purchase orders above **50,000 USD** need a second quote; above **250,000 USD** they need a competitive bid with at least three vendors.
* Multi-year commitments are booked against the budget of the year in which each payment falls.

---

## 4. Close Calendar

* Month-end close completes on **business day 5**. Accruals for unbilled services must be submitted by **business day 2**.
* Quarter-end close completes on **business day 8**, followed by the board pack on business day 12.
