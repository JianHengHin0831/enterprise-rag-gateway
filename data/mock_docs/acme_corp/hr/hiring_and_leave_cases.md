# Acme HR Hiring Approvals & Extended Leave Procedures

> **Confidential — HR Only.** Names and identifiers below are fictional synthetic test data.

---

## 1. Headcount & Offer Approvals

* Every new requisition needs an approved headcount slot in the annual plan. Backfills for resignations are approved by the department director alone.
* Offer approval chain:
  * Offers within the level band midpoint: **HRBP + hiring manager**.
  * Offers between the midpoint and the band maximum: add the **department director**.
  * Offers above the band maximum or with a sign-on bonus over **10,000 USD**: add the **VP of HR** (`HR-OFFER-04`).
* Verbal offers are not allowed before written approval is complete in the HR system.

---

## 2. Background Checks

* Background checks start only after the candidate signs the consent form. The vendor turnaround target is **5 business days**.
* A start date may not be confirmed until the check is cleared, except for interns, whose check may complete within the first **2 weeks** of employment.

---

## 3. Extended Leave

### 3.1 Parental Leave
* Birth parents receive **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave; non-birth parents receive **8 weeks**.
* Leave may be taken in up to **two blocks** within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

### 3.2 Medical Leave
* Medical leave beyond the 5 annual paid sick days requires a physician certificate.
* Up to **12 weeks** of medical leave are paid at **80%** of base salary; beyond that, the case goes to the long-term disability insurer.

### 3.3 Return-to-Work
* HRBP schedules a return-to-work meeting in the employee's first week back. Performance ratings for a quarter with more than **6 weeks** of approved leave are recorded as `N/A`.
