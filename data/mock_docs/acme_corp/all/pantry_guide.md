# Acme Office Pantry Guide (2026 Edition)

Welcome to the Acme Corp pantry and break area. Please help keep shared spaces clean, safe, and welcoming for everyone.

---

## 1. Location & Operating Hours

* **Location**: East side of the **5th floor** (adjacent to the main lounge and quiet booths).
* **Operating Hours**: Open **08:00–21:00**, Monday through Friday.
* **After-Hours & Weekend Access**: Badge access to the 5th floor pantry remains active for employees working authorized overtime, though hot-water boilers and espresso machines enter automatic energy-saving standby mode after 21:00.

---

## 2. Coffee, Tea & Beverage Stations

### 2.1 Espresso & Coffee Machine Schedule
* The commercial espresso machine is deep-cleaned **every Monday morning from 08:00 to 09:30** and is **unavailable during maintenance**. During this window, drip coffee and hot tea remain available at the secondary beverage bar.
* Whole milk, oat milk, and soy milk are restocked every **Tuesday and Thursday morning**. Please return milk cartons to the under-counter chiller immediately after use.
* If the water dispenser displays **`PNT-WTR-02`** (filter replacement alert) or leaks, do not attempt manual resets; report it to Facilities (`facilities@acme-corp.example` / Ext. `4100`).

### 2.2 Cups & Dishware Etiquette
* Ceramic mugs and glassware are provided to minimize single-use waste.
* Please empty coffee grounds or tea bags into the **Compost** bin before placing used mugs in the **"Used Dishes" rack** next to the dishwasher. The dishwasher runs daily at **19:00**.

---

## 3. Refrigerators, Microwaves & Food Storage

### 3.1 Refrigerator Rules & Weekly Cleanout
* **Mandatory Labeling**: Label all personal food and beverage containers stored in the fridge with **your full name and the date placed** using the masking tape and markers mounted on the fridge door.
* **Weekly Cleanout**: All expired or **unlabeled food is cleared and discarded every Friday at 18:00** without exception.
* Reusable lunch bags should fit within the standard shelf height; large catering boxes require prior coordination with Facilities.

### 3.2 Microwave & Heating Guidelines
* Always cover liquids and saucy dishes with the provided microwave splatter guards.
* **Prohibited Items**: Never place metal containers, aluminum foil, or sealed jars inside the microwaves.
* **Odor Courtesy**: Please avoid reheating strongly pungent foods (such as durian or certain fermented seafood) in the 5th-floor pantry microwaves; use the ventilated kitchenette near the B-wing stairwell instead.

---

## 4. Snack Restocking & Dietary Labeling

* **Restock Schedule**: Snacks, fresh fruit, and sparkling water are restocked every **Monday and Thursday at 14:00**.
* **Allergy Safety**: **Top Rack B** is strictly reserved for certified **nut-free and gluten-free snacks**. Do not place personal peanut or tree-nut items on Top Rack B.
* **Requests & Feedback**: Submit snack requests or report empty dispensers in the `#office-pantry` Slack channel or email `facilities@acme-corp.example` (Ext. `4100`).
